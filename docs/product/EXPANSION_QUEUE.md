# Expansion Queue

Last reviewed: 12 September 2026

This is an evidence queue, not a promise of coverage. Each item needs a
recorded source period, demand evidence, graph relationship, data owner and
publication gate before implementation.

## NOW

1. **Vercel storage inventory and retention decision** — reported storage is
   above plan limits; authenticated deployment sizes and IDs are required
   before deletion.
2. **Production measurement and indexing verification** — GA4 DebugView,
   Search Console ownership/reports and weekly records are the missing
   evidence for product and growth decisions.
3. **Ireland source-backed graph gap** — choose one high-intent
   Career/Degree/Education slice only after its program publication evidence is
   ready.

## NEXT

1. **Transparent Degree Match MVP** — only after the Ireland slice provides
   enough connected relationships and comparable dimensions.
2. **Legacy authority bridges** — improve one existing high-impression legacy
   page's contextual route to the canonical graph after GSC review.
3. **Career Future Intelligence cohort** — evaluate one source-ready cohort;
   do not write forecasts before evidence supports uncertainty-aware output.

## LATER

- Country Match after Degree Match evidence.
- Demand-led Career/Degree/Country additions with source-ready graph support.
- Action-layer/job work after legality, freshness, deduplication, mapping and
  maintenance gates.
- Retention, B2B or commercial experiments after Stage 1 evidence.

## NOT YET

- Broad Europe or global country expansion.
- A global university-ranking database.
- A generic job board or recruiting marketplace.
- A paid API, data export, report product, paywall or display advertising.
- New indexable page families generated from search terms alone.
