# Core Decision Engines

Last reviewed: 12 September 2026

These are the permanent product engines. They share the Country ↔ Career ↔
Degree ↔ Education graph and explain evidence, period, source and trade-offs.
No engine may manufacture a score from missing data or replace independent
facts with commercial influence.

| Engine | Current capability | Missing capability | Data dependency | Recommended next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country Intelligence | Public Country pages, country metrics, city context, sources, Path context and Compare surfaces exist. | A deeper, explainable Country → Career → Degree → Education traversal in one validated market. | Comparable country/career, education, cost, tax and work-policy evidence. | Close one Ireland graph gap under its publication gates. |
| Career Future Intelligence | Canonical Career identity, CampCareer Score, evidence, paths and existing demand/pay inputs exist. | A sourced five-year outlook with explicit confidence and uncertainty. | Official projections/trends, current occupation mapping, salary/demand series and defensible interpretation. | Do not predict yet; choose one source-ready cohort after Ireland depth. |
| Degree Match | Existing degree/field discovery, program relationships, Career context and Compare can support exploration. | A transparent preference filter that explains a degree result and its trade-offs. | Maintainable degree → Career → Program/Education relationships plus comparable dimensions. | Build only after the Ireland Golden Slice provides a connected, source-backed subset. |
| Country Match | Country discovery, country comparison and contextual metrics exist. | A transparent preference-based fit view with reasons and trade-offs. | Maintainable cross-country dimensions; no unexplained composite total. | Keep as a later small MVP after Degree Match evidence. |

Each engine must answer “why is this shown?” and distinguish source facts,
CampCareer-created relationships and uncertain/missing evidence. The output is
decision support, not a psychological assessment or deterministic future claim.
