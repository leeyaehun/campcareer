## Required experiment record

Before a candidate moves from observation to a real experiment, create a dated
record with:

1. Hypothesis and specific user problem.
2. Eligible audience and the real action being offered.
3. Commercial model and any price or agreement.
4. Primary revenue or qualified-action measure.
5. Required sample/evidence and observation period.
6. Decision Session, Compare, exit, return, feedback, trust-complaint,
   accessibility and performance guardrails.
7. Disclosure, privacy, source-rights and commercial-independence review.
8. Stop condition, result and decision.

No experiment result is filled in before consented production evidence exists.
