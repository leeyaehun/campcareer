This review confirms source freshness and preservation of history. It does not
authorize a bulk overwrite.

| Data family | Review trigger | Required record | Publication rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Salary and tax | New official period or annual source release | Source/version, period, method, country scope and changed values | Preserve prior period; no comparison across incompatible definitions. |
| Employment, demand and projections | Official release or changed methodology | Source release, occupation mapping, period and confidence | Missing or non-comparable evidence remains unavailable. |
| Tuition, programs and institutions | Academic-year/source release | Provider/program identity, URL, location, currency, status and review date | Do not infer international eligibility, delivery or outcomes. |
| Cost of living | Material source update or annual student guide | Geography, scenario, period and calculation inputs | Keep source-native units and assumptions visible. |
| Visa and work policy | Regulatory change or scheduled review | Official source, effective date, eligibility conditions and reviewer | Treat as Path context; never alter the public Career score. |
| Source licensing | Changed terms or new commercial use | Exact terms, attribution, commercial/redistribution status and reviewer | Follow [source-rights readiness](../business/SOURCE_LICENSING_READINESS.md). |

For every material refresh, use the existing
[DATA_OPERATIONS.md](DATA_OPERATIONS.md) sequence: immutable candidate,
validation, review, controlled publication, public recheck and traceable
`Corrected`, `Updated` or `Source changed` outcome.
